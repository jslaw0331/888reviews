Is online casino legal in Malaysia?

People asking about online casino Malaysia legal status need the statutes, not an offshore logo. Read the Common Gaming Houses Act 1953, the Betting Act 1953, and the Pool Betting Act 1967. An offshore licence is not a Malaysian licence. This page is not legal advice. Check the current official text before you act.

Statutes named in the reprints we checked

On 11 October 2026 we checked public reprints, not a private legal opinion. The controlling text is the current version published by the Attorney General's Chambers. Reprints can lag amendments.

  • Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 (Act 289). The reprint's long title is an Act relating to the suppression of common gaming houses, public gaming, and public lotteries.
  • Betting Act 1953 (Act 495). The reprint's long title is an Act to suppress betting houses and betting in public places.
  • Pool Betting Act 1967 (Act 384). The reprint's long title is an Act to provide for the issue of a licence or the establishment of a Board for the operation or promotion of pool betting and for matters incidental thereto.

The Act 289 reprint also contains a section under which the Minister of Finance may license a company to promote gaming on the terms written in that licence. Whether that section is still in force, and whether it covers a website run from outside Malaysia, has to be read from the current official text. This page does not treat an offshore casino seal as that licence.
